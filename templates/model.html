Why one column was excluded (data leakage)

The dataset includes a self-rated "health condition" column. Students rated "Critical" were always depressed and those rated "Good" never were, so it works like a copy of the answer. With it, ROC-AUC reaches about 0.99, which looks impressive but only reflects the leak. Without it the result is realistic:

Limitations

  • Self-reported survey data from one country; patterns may not carry over to every campus.
  • The model finds correlations, not causes, and its predictive power is modest.
  • It is a screening aid and a learning project, never a diagnosis.
