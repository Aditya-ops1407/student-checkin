People close to you

Tell one person you trust how you have been feeling: a friend, a family member, a teacher or a hostel warden. You do not need the right words. "I have not been okay lately" is enough.

At your college

Many colleges have a counsellor or student welfare cell. Ask your department office where it is. If your college has none, a local doctor or a government hospital's psychiatry department can help.

If someone else is in danger

If you think someone may hurt themselves, stay with them if you can and call 112 or take them to the nearest hospital.
