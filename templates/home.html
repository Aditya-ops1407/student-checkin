Answer a few questions about your studies and the pressures of student life. You get a picture of what affects you most, plus a screening indicator from a model trained on student survey data.

Start the check-in How it works

Free. No sign-up. Your answers are never stored.

How the check-in goes

  1. Tell us a little about you and your studies

    Age, semester, CGPA and a few life details such as who you live with. Nothing that identifies you.

  2. Answer questions about daily life and mood

    Sleep, movement, screens and meals, plus four standard screening questions about mood and worry.

  3. Rate 14 everyday pressures

    Academic, financial and relationship pressure, family expectations, loneliness, worry about the future and more. Each is a quick one-to-five rating.

  4. Read your profile and plan

    See which pressures stand out, what is going well, a personalised plan with a first-week list, and where to find support.

What this is

A learning project that shows how machine learning can pick up patterns in student survey data. It compares your background answers with {{ "{:,}".format(metrics.n_total) }} student responses and tells you how similar your pattern is to students who reported depression.

Everything else you answer (daily life, mood, pressures) is separate. It builds your profile and your personalised plan, so you can see your own priorities.

What this is not

It is not a diagnosis, and it cannot tell whether you have depression or any other condition. Only a qualified professional can do that.

The model is modest (see how well it performs). Treat the result as a prompt to reflect, not a verdict.
